> Historical ledger. The canonical baseline, benchmark contract, and

> append-only evidence and are not active launch instructions.

Primary evidence target: show whether FutureSeed supplies cheap future context
for recurrent reasoners under matched compute, without solver-specific repair or
selector tricks.

accepted table compares four carriers with FutureSeed enabled, so it cannot
measure FutureSeed's causal gain. Add exactly one no-FutureSeed control for

not an architecture sweep: it is the minimum factorial comparison required by
the paper claim that FutureSeed cheaply supplies future context across
recurrent carriers. No seed, geometry, LR, loss, or budget variants are
allowed.

## D. Historical Lessons

- 2026-07-21: A strict official-FLA D192/L10 FutureSeed comparison rejects Linear Attention equation enumeration as the next scaling axis. Installed `flash-linear-attention==0.5.2` files match the pinned wheel byte-for-byte, backend dispatch is disabled, short conv is Triton, and actual CUDA backward nodes are `ChunkGatedDeltaRuleFunctionBackward`, `ChunkKDAFunctionBackward`, and `ChunkGDN2FunctionBackward`; FutureSeed enters only through official `Cache`. At the matched 500-step gate, GDN/KDA/GDN2 pre-fixed holes53 exact is tied at `0.0176`, while CE is `0.9981/1.0252/1.0101` and 46-50 exact is `0.8594/0.6504/0.7539`; all are zero exact at 51-64. GDN2 spends 16% more parameters and about 28% more allocated memory without hard closure, while KDA is slower. Lesson: a newer, more expressive recurrence is not automatically more compute-efficient. Keep official GDN and return to the proven independent-data/long-training scaling path; do not turn this into seed, LR, or three-way no-FS tables.
- 2026-07-16: P-SCALE-034 overturns the premature same-state ceiling diagnosis under the full-diversity regime. Keeping D224/L12/H14/D16 native FutureSeed GDN, loop5/all-loop CE, effective batch128, data, optimizer, and objective fixed, step8000->10000->12000 raises mixed loop5 exact `0.2715->0.3066->0.3730` and official 56-64 exact `0.1621->0.1855->0.2285`. Mixed loop1 remains `0.0234`; loops3/4/5 produce the gain, and hard cases move `31->4->0`, `30->9->0`, `15->7->1`, or `29->12->5` wrong cells. The model was still data/compute limited at roughly one-quarter of a dataset-equivalent draw budget. This does not justify blind endless training: continue unchanged to step16000, then apply the predeclared rule that two successive later gates without a `+0.01` new best stop this state formulation.
- 2026-07-15: P-SCALE-033 isolates FutureSeed under matched long training. At step4500, no-FS remains at CE `1.6372`, fixed holes53/60/64 exact all0, and blank `0.2755/0.2777/0.2766`; FutureSeed reaches CE `0.6415`, exact `0.1055/0.1172/0.1230`, and blank `0.6166/0.6332/0.6133`. Mixed FutureSeed exact rises `0.0234->0.1113` across loop1->5, while no-FS stays zero. The position-thirds diagnostic is mechanism-specific: no-FS remains `0.2085/0.2696/0.3518`, while FutureSeed is nearly flat at `0.6216/0.6193/0.6223`. The predeclared low-ROI rule fires, so stop no-FS at4500 and do not rescue it with seed/LR/loss/noise/width sweeps. This supports cheap future-context initialization and useful recurrent refinement, but not yet superiority over explicit bidirectional/noncausal processing; that quality-throughput-VRAM comparison is the next paper gate.
- 2026-07-15: Joint data-plus-width scaling does not rescue D256/L12. P-SCALE-032 uses the proven 3.83M independent-board corpus, matched curriculum, loop5 all-loop supervision, and 8000 steps. D256 nearly catches D224 at step7000 but finishes lower on holes53/60/64 (`0.2461/0.2656/0.2617` vs `0.2559/0.3066/0.2852`) and on official 51-55/56-64 (`0.4219/0.1562` vs `0.4473/0.1621`). This rejects ordinary width as the next scaling axis, not scaling itself: data diversity was strongly positive in P-SCALE-029. Loop computation remains real, moving mixed exact `0.0234->0.2539` and repairing tens of cells. Stop D288/D320; the next paper-critical evidence is a matched long no-FutureSeed D224 control, then compute-accuracy comparison rather than a parameter-count table.
- 2026-07-12: Supervised loop-count scaling saturates beyond loop5 for the current full-diversity D224/L12 FutureSeed GDN. P-SCALE-031 raises `MAX_LOOPS` from5 to8 and gives all eight loops equal CE supervision from the exact step8000 checkpoint. At step8200, holes53/60/64 loop5->8 exact gains are only `+0.0020/+0.0000/+0.0039`; mixed exact moves `0.2812->0.2871`, while official 51-55/56-64 regress versus P-SCALE-030. Visual hard failures freeze or oscillate after loop5. Lesson: recurrence is essential through loop4/5, but more unrolled passes cannot create missing state information. Stop loop-count tables and invest in one larger full-diversity backbone or more efficient generic recurrent state.
- 2026-07-11: Long-training D256/L12 does not produce a useful width-scaling crossover on official Sudoku. P-SCALE-028 extends the exact step1000 D256 checkpoint to step6000 with cyclic 51-55/51-64/56-64 data and no mechanism changes. Holes53 exact briefly edges D224 at step3000 (`0.0391` vs `0.0352`) but ends far behind at step6000 (`0.0820` vs `0.1680`), even though D256 blank accuracy is slightly higher (`0.6156` vs `0.6120`). Official `51-55` exact is `0.1504` vs D224 `0.2832`, while `56-64` exact only ties around `0.08`. Lesson: simple width spends more FLOPs learning local token accuracy without improving global-consistency efficiency. Do not run D288/D320 tables. The bitter-lesson next axis is more independent data/tokens on the compute-efficient D224 frontier or a genuinely more efficient generic recurrent state, with compute-optimal scaling curves rather than parameter count alone.
- 2026-07-11: Restoring raw FutureSeed state magnitude through a bounded learned RMS gain is not the missing hard-Sudoku mechanism. P-DIAG-027 proves the raw magnitude is board-dependent (`raw RMS sample std 0.0502`), but the learned gain barely follows it (`sample std 0.00310`) and mostly learns a global `0.988x` contraction. Full loop5 exact falls `0.2363 -> 0.2109`; official `51-55` falls `0.3555 -> 0.3418`, and `56-64` falls `0.1582 -> 0.1367`. Loop computation remains strong (`loop1 -> loop5 +0.1875`), so the negative result is specific: a one-dimensional confidence amplitude cannot carry the missing board-wide constraint structure. Do not sweep gain range, initialization, learning rate, or seed.
- 2026-07-07: Doubling effective batch from 128 to 256 is not the missing hard-range stabilizer for D224/L12 native FutureSeed GDN. P-DIAG-026 step12300 holes53/60/64 loop5 exact `0.1562/0.1660/0.1758` was worse than P-DIAG-025 neighboring `0.1875/0.2246/0.2012` and below P-DIAG-021 hard peaks, despite still showing loop gains around `+0.15`. Lesson: the hard exact cliff is not just noisy board-level gradients; next useful work should change state information/content or data scale, not batch-size tables.
- 2026-07-06: Learned loop-update gates are active but not the missing stability mechanism for D224/L12 native FutureSeed GDN. P-DIAG-025 resumed the strongest step12000 checkpoint and the gate moved from about `0.952` to `0.9845/0.9818`, while preserving strong loop gain. But final full exact/blank `0.2266/0.6166` trails P-DIAG-021 `0.2363/0.6287`, and official `56-64` regresses `0.1582 -> 0.1348`. Lesson: changing how much state is overwritten is too weak; do not sweep gate init/bias/temp. Future state work must change what information the loop stores or return to clean curriculum/compute scaling with checkpoint selection.
- 2026-07-04: Hidden-aggregate Gumbel noise as implemented in P-DIAG-023 is a negative structural boundary. The random aggregation collapsed to one-hot token selection (`entropy=0`, `max_weight=1`) and produced enormous perturbations (`norm≈61582`), leaving step500 holes53/60/64 loop5 exact all `0` and blank only `0.257/0.261/0.259`. Lesson: the user intuition that training noise should act through aggregation is plausible, but not via single-token gamble injected into every depth update. Do not sweep noise scale; if revisiting, make it smoother/bounded multi-token aggregation with nonzero entropy.
- 2026-07-05: Bounded soft-group hidden aggregation noise is mechanically healthy but not useful enough. P-DIAG-024 keeps entropy near `0.98`, caps perturbations at norm `2.0`, and trains stably to step1000, but final mixed loop5 exact/blank is only `0.0234/0.5252`, official `51-55` and `56-64` exact are still `0`, and same-step clean D224/L12 is not beaten. Lesson: noise is not the first-order missing piece for the Sudoku transition cliff; pause aggregation-noise knobs and put compute into proven clean scaling or a genuinely better recurrent state/update mechanism.
- 2026-07-04: Cyclic bridge-hard D224/L12 loop5 continuation improves the hardest Sudoku boundary but remains non-monotonic. P-DIAG-021 final official ranges are `46-50 1.0000/1.0000`, `51-55 0.3555/0.7312`, `56-64 0.1582/0.5647`, with strong loop compute (`loop1->5 exact +0.2129`). But holes60/64 checkpoint exact oscillates and stage CE can become tiny without stable full-board exact. Lesson: loop and FutureSeed are doing real computation; the next useful scaling is better recurrent state/capacity or stability, not another same-shape 15000-step repeat.
- 2026-07-04: D224/L16 naive depth scaling is a negative boundary. It fits GPU1 at about `63.7GB`, but reaches step1000 in about `3916s` and only gets loop5 exact/blank `0.0234/0.5192`; official `51-55` and `56-64` exact remain `0`. Lesson: simple extra same-family depth is not the missing global-consistency scale axis; do not run L14/L18/L20 or longer L16 from scratch. Next should use demonstrated data/curriculum slope or a more efficient recurrent state/update formulation.
- 2026-07-02: Mixed hard curriculum is a positive data-scaling axis after D224 step6000. A short 100-step continuation from step6000 on `51-64` reaches holes60 loop5 exact/blank `0.1973/0.6603`, beating the pure `56-64` tail step8000 `0.1836/0.6396`, while holes53 remains useful at `0.1641/0.6342`. Lesson: the failed hard-tail result was likely too narrow a distribution, not proof that harder data is low ROI. Continue with broader hard curriculum (`51-64` or staged mixed hard) before inventing more state-side gates.
- 2026-07-03: Longer mixed `51-64` confirms the data-scaling signal but also shows non-monotonic hard exact. Step6600 hits holes60 loop5 `0.2031/0.6666` and holes53 `0.1816/0.6468`; final step7000 has stronger holes53 `0.1914/0.6568` but holes60 exact eases to `0.1953`. Official `51-55` reaches `0.3105/0.7322`, while `56-64` remains `0.0859/0.5938`. Lesson: broader hard distribution is better than pure `56-64` tail, but same-shape long training alone is not enough for the hardest range; stop blind 9000/12000 repeats.
- 2026-07-02: Naive gated scratch work memory is active but harmful on the D224/L12 transition probe. Step300 scratch diagnostics are nonzero (`scratch_gate=0.1251`, `scratch_delta=0.4044`), yet holes53 loop5 exact/blank is only `0.0000/0.4013` versus fixed D224 step300 `0.0020/0.4704`, and holes60 loop5 is `0.0000/0.4043`.
