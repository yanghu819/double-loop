## D. Historical Lessons

- 2026-07-02: Mixed hard curriculum is a positive data-scaling axis after D224 step6000. A short 100-step continuation from step6000 on `51-64` reaches holes60 loop5 exact/blank `0.1973/0.6603`, beating the pure `56-64` tail step8000 `0.1836/0.6396`, while holes53 remains useful at `0.1641/0.6342`. Lesson: the failed hard-tail result was likely too narrow a distribution, not proof that harder data is low ROI. Continue with broader hard curriculum (`51-64` or staged mixed hard) before inventing more state-side gates.
- 2026-07-03: Longer mixed `51-64` confirms the data-scaling signal but also shows non-monotonic hard exact. Step6600 hits holes60 loop5 `0.2031/0.6666` and holes53 `0.1816/0.6468`; final step7000 has stronger holes53 `0.1914/0.6568` but holes60 exact eases to `0.1953`. Official `51-55` reaches `0.3105/0.7322`, while `56-64` remains `0.0859/0.5938`. Lesson: broader hard distribution is better than pure `56-64` tail, but same-shape long training alone is not enough for the hardest range; stop blind 9000/12000 repeats.
- 2026-07-02: Naive gated scratch work memory is active but harmful on the D224/L12 transition probe. Step300 scratch diagnostics are nonzero (`scratch_gate=0.1251`, `scratch_delta=0.4044`), yet holes53 loop5 exact/blank is only `0.0000/0.4013` versus fixed D224 step300 `0.0020/0.4704`, and holes60 loop5 is `0.0000/0.4043`. Lesson: the bottleneck is not solved by bolting a small per-cell scratch state onto loop context; do not sweep scratch bias/noise/gaussian. Future state-dynamics work needs a more integrated recurrent formulation or a data/curriculum axis with demonstrated slope.
- 2026-07-02: D256/L12 width scaling is trainable but low ROI under the current native FutureSeed GDN formulation. It fits GPU1 at microbatch32/grad_accum4 and reaches lower CE than D224 around step650, but step1000 board metrics do not improve: holes53 loop5 exact/blank `0.0176/0.5150` vs D224 `0.0176/0.5284`, holes60 loop5 `0.0215/0.5308`. Lesson: after D224/L12 opens the transition, simply making the token/channel path wider is not the next scaling axis; invest in a more efficient recurrent state/update formulation or curriculum/data scaling with demonstrated slope, not D256/D320 width tables.

- 2026-06-21: official EqR Maze token accuracy is dominated by non-PATH cells; path-aware metrics and visualizations are mandatory.
- 2026-06-21: a generic path-token-weighted objective opens official EqR Maze path recovery, but the failure shape becomes high-recall broad masks, not solved paths.
- 2026-06-21: FutureSeed is neutral on official EqR once path recovery is rewarded; treat EqR as a strong noncausal baseline/boundary, not as the backbone where FutureSeed should win.
- 2026-06-21: causal RWKV on official path-weight Maze also converges to the same broad-mask solution, so this proxy/objective does not isolate the missing-future-context mechanism.
- 2026-06-24: if FutureSeed is proposed as a cheap bidirectional mixer, the scan implementation is part of the claim. A Python loop over sequence positions is not acceptable: it stalled under `torch.compile` and was too slow without compile. The first viable path was vectorized/parallel-prefix scan; follow-up commit `d9c2600` added a Triton CUDA custom-autograd scan that matches prefix outputs/gradients and is about `12.2x` faster on the microcheck after warmup.
- 2026-06-21: adding a generic PATH/non-PATH boundary objective makes broad masks costly, but the failure changes to overpruning true path cells. FutureSeed mildly improves recall/F1 under this pressure, yet loop gain is still near zero; do not turn this into loss-weight sweeps.
- 2026-06-21: FutureSeed has a strong causal/RWKV Sudoku mechanism result, but official EqR Maze support is not established.
- 2026-06-21: no selector, repair, search, or maze-specific postprocessing should be used to rescue Maze scores.
- 2026-06-22: H96 official EqR compression gate is also broad-mask neutral: FutureSeed delta `-0.000178`, recall `1.0`, FP about `271`/case. Do not continue hidden-size/gate/seed sweeps; Maze needs generic recurrent denoising/attractor training or a different output objective.
- 2026-06-22: generic denoising-attractor feedback is also broad-mask neutral on official Maze30. no-FS and FS both hit the step300 kill rule with negative loop gain and FP increasing from loop1 to loop16. DAT teaches a fixed point for the existing wide mask, not late-loop correction; do not sweep corruption mix, DAT weight, or seeds.
- 2026-06-22: Maze abortable probes now preserve hard-case evidence. `rwkv_maze_probe.py` writes `abort.json`, partial metrics, README, and loop1/4/8/16 hard-case HTML before returning on broad-mask kill. The verification run also confirmed the failure shape: loop16 copied loop1 exactly at step100 with recall `1.0` and FP `272.1`/case.
- 2026-06-23: Pure Maze scale has a real but insufficient loop signal. D256/L12 train8/eval16 cannot run no-checkpoint on A800 80GB even at batch14. Activation-checkpoint b64 with lr1e-4 is stable and loop16 cuts about `44` FP/case with zero FN at step100-200, but the mask remains extremely broad (`pred_path_frac≈0.71`) and F1 plateaus around `0.314`. - 2026-06-23: Expanding RWKV recurrent state by increasing `head_dim` from 32 to 64 is a real capacity signal but a bad throughput axis. - 2026-06-23: The old official EqR FutureSeed patch is not a strong cheap-bidirectional test by itself: it injects H/L latent state into the other level, but under causal attention it does not create an independent future-token source. Its neutral Maze causal/compression results should be interpreted as a boundary for that add-on, not a verdict on the broader FutureSeed-as-cheap-bidirectional mechanism.

- 2026-06-24: Cheap-bidirectional FutureSeed on official EqR Sudoku gives a real state-dynamics signal but not a score win at tiny budget. Under `arch.mlp_t=false` causal attention, reverse-causal FutureSeed lowers `residual_of_16_steps` from `167.90` to `82.85` and raises token accuracy by `+0.00649`, while exact remains `0` and eval loss is slightly worse. - 2026-06-24: The longer cheap-bidirectional scale gate strengthens the mechanism boundary. FutureSeed's early residual advantage reproduces at step250 but shrinks sharply by step500 (`-84.42` residual16 delta to `-9.80`), exact stays `0`, and loss is not better.
