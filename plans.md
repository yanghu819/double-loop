# Experiment Plans

Primary evidence target: show whether FutureSeed supplies cheap future context
for recurrent reasoners under matched compute, without solver-specific repair or
selector tricks.

## D. Historical Lessons

- 2026-06-21: official EqR Maze token accuracy is dominated by non-PATH cells; path-aware metrics and visualizations are mandatory.
- 2026-06-21: a generic path-token-weighted objective opens official EqR Maze path recovery, but the failure shape becomes high-recall broad masks, not solved paths.
- 2026-06-21: FutureSeed is neutral on official EqR once path recovery is rewarded; treat EqR as a strong noncausal baseline/boundary, not as the backbone where FutureSeed should win.
- 2026-06-21: causal RWKV on official path-weight Maze also converges to the same broad-mask solution, so this proxy/objective does not isolate the missing-future-context mechanism.
- 2026-06-24: if FutureSeed is proposed as a cheap bidirectional mixer, the scan implementation is part of the claim. A Python loop over sequence positions is not acceptable: it stalled under `torch.compile` and was too slow without compile. The first viable path was vectorized/parallel-prefix scan; follow-up commit `d9c2600` added a Triton CUDA custom-autograd scan that matches prefix outputs/gradients and is about `12.2x` faster on the microcheck after warmup.
- 2026-06-21: adding a generic PATH/non-PATH boundary objective makes broad masks costly, but the failure changes to overpruning true path cells. FutureSeed mildly improves recall/F1 under this pressure, yet loop gain is still near zero; do not turn this into loss-weight sweeps.
- 2026-06-21: FutureSeed has a strong causal/RWKV Sudoku mechanism result, but official EqR Maze support is not established.
- 2026-06-21: no selector, repair, search, or maze-specific postprocessing should be used to rescue Maze scores.
- 2026-06-22: H96 official EqR compression gate is also broad-mask neutral: FutureSeed delta `-0.000178`, recall `1.0`, FP about `271`/case. Do not continue hidden-size/gate/seed sweeps; Maze needs generic recurrent denoising/attractor training or a different output objective.
- 2026-06-22: generic denoising-attractor feedback is also broad-mask neutral on official Maze30. no-FS and FS both hit the step300 kill rule with negative loop gain and FP increasing from loop1 to loop16. DAT teaches a fixed point for the existing wide mask, not late-loop correction; do not sweep corruption mix, DAT weight, or seeds.
- 2026-06-22: Maze abortable probes now preserve hard-case evidence. `rwkv_maze_probe.py` writes `abort.json`, partial metrics, README, and loop1/4/8/16 hard-case HTML before returning on broad-mask kill. The verification run also confirmed the failure shape: loop16 copied loop1 exactly at step100 with recall `1.0` and FP `272.1`/case.
- 2026-06-23: Pure Maze scale has a real but insufficient loop signal. D256/L12 train8/eval16 cannot run no-checkpoint on A800 80GB even at batch14. Activation-checkpoint b64 with lr1e-4 is stable and loop16 cuts about `44` FP/case with zero FN at step100-200, but the mask remains extremely broad (`pred_path_frac≈0.71`) and F1 plateaus around `0.314`. Scale alone is not the Maze fix under the current path-weight CE objective.
- 2026-06-23: Expanding RWKV recurrent state by increasing `head_dim` from 32 to 64 is a real capacity signal but a bad throughput axis. State64 b32 moved official Maze30 from the state32 broad-mask regime (`loop16 F1 0.3144`, pred_frac `0.7097`, FP `519.4`) to the EqR-like path-weight regime (`loop16 F1 0.4686`, pred_frac `0.4322`, FP `269.7`, FN `0`), but loop1 and loop16 were identical at step100/200. Lesson: recurrent state capacity can fix the operating point, but it still does not make later loops correct mistakes; future scale should prefer head_dim32 with more heads/width or a simple learned decision state, not head_dim64.
- 2026-06-23: Official EqR Maze-Unique released-checkpoint reproduction is now established: D16/B1 `0.827` vs paper `0.822`, D64/B1 `0.892` vs `0.889`, and D64/B128 convergence top-1 `0.944444` vs `0.930`. The key reproduction setting is Maze `noise_scale=0.01`; generic `0.5` destroys exact accuracy while token accuracy remains misleadingly high.
- 2026-06-23: Official EqR Sudoku-Extreme quick gate is now reproduced on GPU1: seed0 D64/B128/noise0.5/top4 gives top1 `0.991699`, top4 exact `0.986816`, majority exact `0.987305`, any-correct `0.992676` over 2048 examples, matching the README quick target. Full paper-scale Sudoku remains a separate 8-GPU 423,168-example job; do not claim it was run.
- 2026-06-23: The old official EqR FutureSeed patch is not a strong cheap-bidirectional test by itself: it injects H/L latent state into the other level, but under causal attention it does not create an independent future-token source. Its neutral Maze causal/compression results should be interpreted as a boundary for that add-on, not a verdict on the broader FutureSeed-as-cheap-bidirectional mechanism.
- 2026-06-24: Stop low-ROI baseline seed completion once the next mechanism decision is supported. Official Maze paper-point reproduction plus Sudoku quick seed0/1/2 in-range is enough to move to FutureSeed; continuing seeds 3/4 would only fill a table and delay the actual hypothesis test.
- 2026-06-24: Cheap-bidirectional FutureSeed on official EqR Sudoku gives a real state-dynamics signal but not a score win at tiny budget. Under `arch.mlp_t=false` causal attention, reverse-causal FutureSeed lowers `residual_of_16_steps` from `167.90` to `82.85` and raises token accuracy by `+0.00649`, while exact remains `0` and eval loss is slightly worse. The next valid test is one longer matched-compute scale gate; more seeds at this budget are low ROI.
- 2026-06-24: The longer cheap-bidirectional scale gate strengthens the mechanism boundary. FutureSeed's early residual advantage reproduces at step250 but shrinks sharply by step500 (`-84.42` residual16 delta to `-9.80`), exact stays `0`, and loss is not better. This is evidence that the current FutureSeed injection moves recurrent state early but does not by itself create better decisions. Stop more epochs/seeds for this injection; inspect residual-to-exact mismatch or change generic state/readout dynamics.
- 2026-06-24: Correction: the official EqR mixer-replacement experiment was an off-mainline bidirectional scan mixer, not FutureSeed. FutureSeed means cross-layer terminal-state seeding. Do not rename a right-to-left scan as FutureSeed. The scan mixer had early sample-efficiency but failed the e1024 gate (`base acc=0.6644/exact=0.0249`, scan acc=0.4231/exact=0`) and is archived as a side negative result only.
- 2026-06-24: Native FutureSeed-RWKV terminal-state seeding is integrated into official EqR and runs through the CUDA statepassing path, but the first fair no-FS vs FS Sudoku gate is neutral/negative. At h192/head6/e64, no-FS vs native-FS gives eval acc `0.1651596` vs `0.1651476`, exact both `0`, lm loss `2.370409` vs `2.369608`, residual16 `222.3120` vs `222.3994`, and FS eval is `1.67x` slower. Lesson: the corrected FutureSeed definition is trainable but not yet a strong EqR mixer replacement; do not sweep seed/gate, change the generic state dynamics if continuing.
- 2026-06-25: Full standalone FutureSeed-RWKV-loop backbone is a different result from the failed 2-layer official EqR mixer transplant. On official EqR Sudoku arrays at D192/L10/loop5/batch128/600 steps, no-FS plateaued at CE `1.6486`, exact `0`, blank_acc `0.2618`; FS reached CE `1.0632`, exact `0.0137`, blank_acc `0.4972`, and loop1->loop5 exact `0.0015->0.0137`. Lesson: FutureSeed provides real optimization/opening value for the original backbone under official data; next scale this clean path, and fix `FULL_ROLLOUT_KS=` so empty means no rollout rather than default rollout metrics.
- 2026-06-25: Matched 1500-step official Sudoku scale strengthens the FutureSeed mechanism but bounds same-config epoch scaling. FS reaches loop5 exact `0.0293`, blank_acc `0.5291`, and loop1->loop5 exact gain `+0.0278`; matched no-FS remains exact `0`, blank_acc `0.2697`, and CE `1.6312`. Lesson: FutureSeed is the optimization/opening source in the full backbone, but more epochs at D192/L10 are not enough for strong Sudoku. Next ROI is capacity/data/curriculum or a simple generic FutureSeed state-dynamics change, not another same-size long run.
