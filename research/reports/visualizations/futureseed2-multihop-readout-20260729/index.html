Mechanism conclusion

  • This is not a broken implementation: zero-init identity and the direct state-read formula both have maximum error 0; the new scale receives gradient 0.125; all ten training layers use official FLA GDN2 with Triton convolution and no fallback.
  • Loops 1 and 2 are numerically tied in aggregate. The quality loss begins at loop3, exactly when the extra residual reaches its largest norm. The added path interferes with established refinement rather than improving initial opening.
  • The result is board-dependent: case 175 improves, while case 17 is turned from solved into five wrong cells. A fixed two-hop residual is not a reliable source of correction.
  • Training CE is unchanged while full-board exact falls. Ordinary token loss does not distinguish a useful cross-depth message from one that disrupts global closure.
  • Do not sweep hop, scale, seed, LR, loss, or length. Cross-more-layers and raw block carry are both closed. A later FutureSeed2 must improve how future evidence is formed, not merely send existing terminal state farther or keep it longer.
