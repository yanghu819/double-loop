Decision: pure scale produced a real loop-pruning signal, but by step200 the model was stuck in an even wider recall-preserving mask. Continue blindly to step800 was stopped as low ROI. The next mechanism should keep the generic scaling spirit but change how the loss or state turns pruning pressure into a sharper decision boundary.
