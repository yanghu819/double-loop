What changed

rope_scale=0 is checked bit-exact against the frozen plain SDPA output. RoPE adds no trainable parameter and the attention remains fully noncausal. This experiment tests whether the failed Transformer ceiling lacked a translation-invariant relative address for the adjacent key/value relation.
